Face ID Enrollment

Set up single-snapshot Face ID for attendance and access

Learners can enroll with one clear camera snapshot. After camera access is allowed, the system captures a single image, confirms the enrollment, and uses that approved snapshot to create a face template for attendance check-in or controlled access workflows.

Recommended backend stack: FastAPI or Flask for the API, OpenCV for camera/image capture handling, and face_recognition or insightface for face detection plus embedding generation.

  1. 1
    Allow camera access

    Grant permission so the enrollment page can open your device camera securely in the browser.

  2. 2
    Capture one clear snapshot

    Center your face, face the camera directly, and take one well-lit image with no blur or heavy obstruction.

  3. 3
    Confirm enrollment

    Review the snapshot preview and approve it so the system can create your face template for future attendance and access checks.

Tip: use a steady camera, neutral background, and even lighting for the best match quality.

Status: previewing the planned learner flow for camera access, single-snapshot capture, and enrollment confirmation.
